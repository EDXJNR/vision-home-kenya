Meet Marilyn

Founder, Vision Homes Kenya

I'm Marilyn, a Nairobi-based real estate advisor and the founder of Vision Homes Kenya. I've been in real estate since 2021, when selling my first piece of land pulled me into the industry and I never left. That path eventually led me to start Vision Homes Kenya, where I get to help people find homes that actually fit their lives, not just their budget on paper.

I specialize in Ngong, Kikuyu, Muhuri Road, Syokimau, and Westlands, areas I know well and follow closely, so when I show you a listing, it's because I'd stand behind it myself.

Every property I list is personally inspected before it reaches you. I believe in being upfront, including the parts that aren't perfect, because a house you fully understand is one you'll actually be happy in.
