About Vision Homes Kenya

Founded in Nairobi in 2021 — with a single mission: to make finding the perfect home effortless, transparent, and even enjoyable.

Founded 2021

Built on trust,
driven by results.

Vision Homes Kenya was born from a frustration shared by thousands of Nairobi homebuyers: the search for a quality home was opaque, stressful and riddled with unreliable information. Our founders set out to build something different.

Today we are Nairobi's most trusted luxury agency, with a dedicated team of specialists covering every prime neighborhood. We have placed over 2,400 families and handled transactions exceeding KES 5 Million.

Our promise is simple: we will never show you a property we have not personally visited and verified, and we will work tirelessly until you find the right fit — not just any fit.
